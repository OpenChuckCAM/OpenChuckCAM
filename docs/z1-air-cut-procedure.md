# Makera Z1 air-cut procedure (gate 4)

**Status: reviewed and accepted by the project owner, who operates the Z1 (14 Oct 2026), with no changes** (work breakdown slice 20 items 10 and 12; [decision 0031](decisions/0031-slice-20-packaging-and-trial-documents.md), OI-133, closed). **Updated on 22 Nov 2026 to the 0.55.0-rc3 test findings, with the owner's approval relayed by the coordinator** (see the change note at the end). Gate 4 has been run with this procedure on one Z1, firmware 1.1.3, for 0.55.0-rc2 (11 to 14 Nov 2026, issue #586) and for 0.55.0-rc3 (16 to 22 Nov 2026, issue #586, three comments; archived under [`docs/archive/0653-release-0-55-verification/`](archive/0653-release-0-55-verification/README.md) for [decision 0653](decisions/0653-verification-report-for-release-0-55-0.md)). Those runs are evidence for that machine and those builds; they do not make any program safe. **Generated G-code is the user's to verify before running on a machine (NFR-12): read each program before you run it.**

This is gate 4 of [verification plan section 6](verification-plan.md), using the reference jobs in [reference-jobs.md](reference-jobs.md). It extends the SP-12 air cut ([decision 0007](decisions/0007-sp-12-z1-conventions.md); `spikes/sp12/AIR-CUT.md` on `spike/sp-12-z1-conventions`), which used a hand-written program, to programs generated by the application. Read the [safe-practice checklist](z1-safe-practice-checklist.md) first and complete its sections 1 and 2.

**Step-by-step instructions** for gates 4 and 5, with one-page checklists and results forms, are in [z1-gates-4-5-test-instructions.md](z1-gates-4-5-test-instructions.md) (lane GATES, draft for the owner's review). Step 3 was corrected on 22 Oct 2026 to time a whole operation (OI-910, the owner's answer), and step 0 records the owner's answers on the spindle and `T1 M6` (OI-911, OI-912). That document has not been updated to the rc3 findings (OI-2154): where it differs from this procedure, follow this one.

**Rules (verification plan section 6).** Attended, emergency stop in reach, **no tool, no material**, reduced feed. Any unexpected motion stops the trial and is logged as a defect with the G-code attached.

## What gate 4 must show

1. The controller accepts every line of each program and raises no alarm.
2. The motion matches the simulator run (gate 3) and the A direction, wrap and unwind behave as decision 0007 recorded (rechecked on firmware 1.1.3, decision 0649 item 3).
3. One whole operation takes the time the application predicts for the Z1 feed rule, with A at most 1800 deg/min (decision 0647), scaled for the override, within a band set at the first run (decision 0007: within about 10 % confirmed the rule in the SP-12 run; the rc2 and rc3 runs used a band of 10 %, rc2 ratios 0.97 to 1.10).
4. The `G93` result on the installed firmware is recorded (SP-12 found it ignored on 1.1.2, and it is ignored on 1.1.3; the Z1 post never writes it).

## Steps

Run with **feed override at 10 %** unless the step says otherwise. Watch the first 20 lines of each program with a hand near the stop. Stop on anything unexpected. (The rc3 spot checks ran at 100 % because the same programs, with only F words and safe-Z words changed, had passed at reduced feed on rc2; a program not yet air-cut starts at 10 %.)

## Pass and fail

- **Pass** (verification plan gate 4): motion matches the simulator; the controller accepts every command; no alarms; the measured time is within the band for the Z1 feed rule (agree the band at step 3, starting from the 10 % used in the SP-12, rc2 and rc3 runs).
- **Fail:** any unexpected motion, alarm or rejected line. Stop, note the line, attach the program and the application commit to a defect, and do not go on to gate 5. A change to a post or to the kinematics sends the programs back to gate 1.

## After the air cut

Gate 5 (the first cut in wax, foam or tooling board) follows only when gate 4 has passed and the checklist (sections 3 and 4) has been reviewed by the operator. The first-cut limit is ±0.1 mm, with no 3-axis test part first ([decision 0221](decisions/0221-gate-5-first-cut-project-and-limit.md)). The tool change was tested attended (OI-41, closed 14 Nov 2026) and with a real tool at the rc3 first cut (E-03).

Before gate 5, with the program's own tool fitted and measured (no material, stop in reach, 10 % override), check the spindle at the first safe-Z rapid: with the tool L mm out of the collet face, the expected machine Z is −73.000 + L + 45 at safe Z 45, which must stay below the −1.0 soft limit; any tool with L up to 27 mm does (decision 0649 Verification; rc3 record part 1, B-08). A check with no tool fitted (−28.0) shows nothing about that margin. The figure holds for the owner's Z1 with the tool length offset calibrated to the collet face.

Found in the rc3 runs (rc3 record part 2, C2; machine behaviour, not software):

- `G10 L2 P1` with a Z word also cleared the tool length offset (`TL0`), which moves the work Z by that amount. Check `$#` after any `G10 L2`.
- After setting G54 by hand, the Makera Controller's work-origin and 4th-axis set-up buttons set G54 again. Read G54 with `$#` at the start of each session.
- The A axis on the owner's Z1 is tilted against the X travel (about 0.0004 to 0.0007 mm/mm vertically and 0.0022 to 0.0041 mm/mm horizontally) and could not be adjusted by hand; for the rc3 first cut G54 Y and Z were set from a probe measurement of the axis at the part (Y −124.136, Z −39.710). The alignment limits used are proposals, not Makera specifications. This matters for gate 5, not for the air cut.
