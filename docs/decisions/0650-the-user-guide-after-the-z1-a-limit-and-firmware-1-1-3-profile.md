## Decision

The owner's choices, relayed by the coordinator on 15 Nov 2026:

1. **The tutorial moves the part past the chuck** (option A). In tutorial step 4, before the stock is chosen, the reader sets **Part position > Offset along X** to 18 and **Work offset and clearance > Zero at X** to 18, so the stock fills in at X 17 and the G-code is as before; the finished tutorial project matches the example project (17.990234375 mm, decision 0649 item 12) to within 0.01 mm. The program does not warn when the stock starts behind the headstock face; the coordinator files that product gap as a ticket, not in this lane.
2. **18 mm is not a fact for other chucks.** Tutorial step 4 has a Warning box (a too-small value puts the path inside the chuck, which the program does not check; `user-docs/STYLE.md`, Safety): measure the distance from the face of the headstock body to the chuck face where the stock starts, with your jaws fitted and gripping, and use it for **Offset along X** and **Zero at X**; the program's X0 is set on the machine with the controller's work offset (G54). The same wording is a Note in the worked examples on the baluster project, in `reference/machine-profiles.md` (rotary reference) and in `reference/gcode-and-posts.md` (work offset), and the glossary's work offset says it is set with the controller.
3. **The tutorial's tool warning stays and is explained** (decision 0649 item 8, option A): one paragraph in step 7 and one export step in step 10, because the warning is a possible collision that the export asks to acknowledge.
4. **Known issues in the 0.55.0 release notes**, in the owner's wording: Facing on STEP parts (#800) and face flow on a closed shape (#791). `docs/releasing.md` gains a checklist line for known issues.

Consequences of decision 0649 that change steps (accepted by the coordinator, 15 Nov 2026): the tabs and held regions example sets the stock's **Start X** to 4.99 (13 mm before the part at 17.99) and its grip to 14 mm (13 mm leaves a head zone of 0.0002 mm because of the shift of 17.990234375 mm); the tutorial's export ticks a second box.
