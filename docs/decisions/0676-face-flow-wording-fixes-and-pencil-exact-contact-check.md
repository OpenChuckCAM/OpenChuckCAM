## Context and decision

1. **#631, two pass counts.** Face-flow finishing's first note counted the pieces of passes left after the unreached stretches were taken out ("cuts 12 passes on 1 face"), and the face's note the passes planned across the face ("in 17 passes"). Side by side they read as a contradiction. The first note now counts the passes that cut, one per line across a face however many pieces its unreached stretches split it into, and a face note whose planned passes do not all cut says how many did: "face 20 is cut from its guide, edge 65 (49.1 mm long), towards the opposite side in 17 passes up to 0.953 mm apart along the face; 12 of them cut and the rest are left out: along them the tool reaches no part of the face it may cut". On the blisk floors of the guide's examples and of the `face_flow` reference job the number of pieces equalled the number of lines, so the first note's numbers do not change there.
2. **#614, the empty Cut on line.** The Geometry section said "With nothing in Cut on, the operation cuts the whole part." for every strategy, though swarf and face-flow finishing refuse an empty Cut on. The line now comes from `openchuck_app::operation::empty_cut_on_hint`, which says for those two that they cut only the faces chosen and are not generated with no face set in Cut on. The refusal and the line read the same list of strategies (`needs_chosen_faces`), so they cannot disagree.
3. **#615, counts and the STEP notice.** A selection set's count agrees with its noun in the tree, the set inspector and the Geometry section ("1 face", "1 edge"), through `SelectionSummary::counted`. The STEP import notice names face-flow finishing among the strategies that read the exact faces (decision 0525 D5).
4. **#632, pencil clean-up with exact contact.** The report (main 9dae85d3): with a 2 mm flat end at the shouldered shaft's corner (X 11), 6 passes and 40 moves with Exact STEP surface contact off and 31 passes and 390 moves with it on. **Reproduced on current `main`** with the check's own project (`owner-checks/351-257-exact-step-contact` on branch `owner-checks/2026.1-alpha`, public part, synthetic: 26 mm stock, 6 mm held, axial finishing with a 3.175 mm tapered ball of tip radius 0.5 before the pencil, the default 0.15 mm least material left): off 6 passes and 40 moves, on 31 and 390 (LinuxCNC). The pencil's own setting makes the difference: with it on for the axial operation only, 6 passes; for the pencil only, 31. The part is turned, and the tapered ball leaves nearly the same thin ridge in the corner all the way round (varying only between its passes, about 3.6 degrees apart there), yet with the setting off the pencil cuts 7 degrees of the turn, three of its six passes being single points (a plunge into the corner and straight out), and with it on 316 degrees in 31 stretches, none a single point. The 0.15 mm threshold is not the cause: the same project with the least material left at 0.10 and 0.20 mm gives the same 6 passes off and 31 on. Lane PENCIL-REST (draft pull request #856) found the mechanism: pencil judges the material left from the drop's contact point, which for a flat end resting on a line is arbitrary (the tessellation reports the flat's far edge, about 1.5 mm or more from the corner's material, at the edge of the tool's reach; the exact faces report points anywhere along the line), and the rest is bisected flush against the wall, where a drop of the same tool can read the graze as resting on the shoulder above. Without an operation before it (cylinder or bounding-box stock, alone or after rotary roughing) the pencil cuts the whole corner in one pass either way, and a kernel test holds that. What to do about it is a design question: the owner decided on 23 Nov 2026 (relayed by the coordinator, decision 0049) that it goes to a separate design lane, PENCIL-REST, which reads these numbers and the check's project, and that this pull request changes no pencil behaviour. The user guide's known limitations and the release notes say pencil clean-up can cut only part of a corner after a finishing operation leaves a thin ridge there, and to check the corner in the simulation. Issue #632 stays open for that lane.

## Alternatives considered

- **#631: count pieces in both notes.** Not chosen: a pass split by an unreached stretch would count twice, and the face note's count is the planned spacing across the face, which the user sets with the stepover.
- **#614: hide the line for those strategies.** Not chosen: the line is where the user learns what an empty Cut on does; for these two it should say what it does.
