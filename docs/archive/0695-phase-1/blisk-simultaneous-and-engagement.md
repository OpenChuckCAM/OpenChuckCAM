## How the runs were made

The CLI still cannot add an operation (no subcommand), so each run copies a project and writes an operation file by hand (`operations/c0ffee00-....toml`, strategy `simultaneous_finishing`, empty or given `[parameters]`), then:

```
openchuck-cli regenerate P --post makera-z1 --tool-numbers keep --out P/out --acknowledge-partial-path --acknowledge-collision-warnings
openchuck-cli simulate P          # cell 0.1 mm, fails on overcut beyond one cell
```

- **STL**: `new`, copy `testdata/stl/blisk.stl` to `models/part.stl`, `import`, `machine makera-z1`, `setup --new "Setup 1" --part models/part.stl --stock cylinder=42,52,-1 --material oak` (as OI-318), tools copied from blisk-start (3 mm flat end, 3 mm ball, 16 mm flutes, oak, stepover 1). Part X 0 to 50: disc X 0 to 10, shoulder at X 10, hub r 15 to 9, blades to r 20.
- **STEP**: copy of `testdata/examples/blisk-start/blisk-project` (schema 27, part X 27.99 to 77.99 in model frame: disc to X 37.99, shoulder at X 37.99). Exact contact is on by default for the ball.
- Defaults: pattern spiral, max_angle_step 5, max_tilt 10, lead 0, tolerance default, stepover from tool (1 mm).

## Causes (code, main d1674b7)

All in `crates/openchuck-path/src/simultaneous.rs` unless noted.

1. **Tip-crossing jumps are tested at only one pose after the last halving.** `refine_from` tests the middle, both quarter points and the long-move poses only while `depth < MAX_DEPTH` (2438-2475). After that (2484-2519) only the middle pose's drop is read; a jump (`travel > reach`, 2437) is split through the raised middle pose (2489-2495) and each piece is again checked at its middle only. A move that rounds a sharp convex edge (blade tip, rim) turns A 50-90 deg in one block; the tool is clear at its middle but cuts the edge near one end, which no pose tests. Also `None => {}` (2541): a middle with no node keeps the move untested when there is no region constraint. (Which of the two let line 22888 through was not instrumented: changing source in the shared checkout was avoided.)
2. **The hidden-surface move test reads a flat end's corner contact as no cut.** `deviation` on a contour (2253-2258) returns `hidden::to_tool(profile, across, along).min(offset)`, the distance from the tool profile of the one point the drop came to rest on. A flat end rests on its corner; shifted back along the axis by the drop offset that point lies on the cylinder side, so `to_tool` (`simultaneous/hidden.rs:526-533`) is about 0 whatever the offset. Moves in which the flat end sweeps through a blade tip pass (1.3-2.5 mm overcuts on STL and STEP). A ball's side is round, so it reads partly (STL ball 0.058 mm).
3. **The seed follows the drive normal, which jumps at a sharp edge** (OI-337 item 1, unchanged except the flat-bottom blend): `seed_tip` (2181-2216) puts the tool's point with the normal on the drive point; at a blade tip the ray passes from the tip face to the side face between two samples 1e-7 apart, so consecutive nodes are 15-25 mm and 50-90 deg of A apart. `smoothed_normal` falls back to the facet normal across an edge (1975, `dot > 0.5`), so there is no smoothing to soften the jump. The narrow flat-bottom blend (2206) does not apply to the contour passes' corner flip seen in the G-code (tip X jumps by the radius).
4. **The axial-facing shoulder and the blade-root corners are out of reach of any A.** The X-slope note (`note`, 2688-2692) reports up to 57-65 deg at the shoulder; the shoulder gets one turn (`faces.rs`, decision 0297) and the hidden-surface contours (decision 0368) but the concave root corner of shoulder + hub + blade within a 3 mm tool is unreachable (unreached areas). This is the residue of 2.5 mm the simulation reports.
5. **The "largest tilt used" note over-reads at edges**: `findings.tilt` (2695) is |phi - phi_n|, where phi_n is the drive normal at the node, which at a blade edge is the other face's normal; 83-86 deg is the edge jump, not the tool's lean from the surface it cuts (OI-318's SIM4AX note).
6. Material left warnings (0.57-1.47 mm, `out.under` 2516, `under_warning` 1026) come from the same edge jumps (the move leaves material where it skips round the edge).
7. Unrelated defects found on the way: the Makera Z1 post's A unwind fails the replay check on the axial pattern (LinuxCNC fine); spurious off-axis zones (OP-26, decision 0363 item 3) on 0.05 mm strips of the STEP blisk.

## Options for #710

- **A. Make the move test sound at edges, and document the limit (S/M, 3-4 days).** Test quarter and long-move poses at every depth, including after the raises; treat a missing middle as a failed test, not a pass; in the contour branch read the cut as the larger of the profile distance and the drop offset along the axis (or the drop offset alone, as on visible surfaces with `MIN_ALONG`). Where a move still cuts after the raises, break the pass (link) instead of keeping it, so the blade tips are skipped rather than gouged, with a warning naming the edges. User guide and refusal/notice text for the blisk: what is finished, the root/shoulder residue, the workaround (region or selection to the blades and hub, rest machining or a smaller ball at the roots, helical or face-flow on the shoulder). Goldens: none expected (lobed, cone references have no sharp edges; to be confirmed by the golden suite); blisk output changes (gouges become breaks). Risk: more breaks or refusals on parts with edges (crankshaft webs); run time up somewhat. No public API change.
- **B. A + roll round convex edges (M, 6-9 days).** Detect a sharp convex edge where consecutive drive normals jump past a threshold (the facet-fallback signal at 1975 or the node normals), and insert nodes that pivot the tool about the edge (contact fixed on the edge, A turning, seed continuous) so the move between faces is a roll, not a chord; apply the OI-337 continuous seed only there. Blade tips and the rim are then finished instead of skipped. Goldens: unchanged for parts without sharp convex edges (lobed, cone); STEP crankshaft and blisk outputs change. Risk: edge detection thresholds on meshes (facets read as edges), more moves. No public API change (private `Plan` methods).
- **C. General continuous seed plus a clearance-query move test (L, 2-3 weeks).** OI-337 item 1 in full (seed from the track position and planned axis everywhere) and a penetration test of the swept tool at each test pose instead of drop offsets. Changes every simultaneous program (lobed reference golden, e2e goldens, user-guide figures). A clearance query on `SurfaceSource` (public trait, `surface.rs:141`) as a new required method is breaking: workspace minor bump and changelog (decision 0048); a provided default method avoids the break but must be correct for both meshes and exact faces. Risk highest; benefit spreads to all parts.

Recommendation: A now (it closes an OP-12 gap that simulation already exposes, and lets #710 close with "documented setting + recorded limit"), B as the 2026.2 stretch if the owner wants the tips finished in one operation; C only with the owner's agreement to change every program.

Done-criterion status today: STL blisk + 3 mm ball at defaults passes simulation (documentable). STEP blisk (the example project) does not: 0.91 mm at the blade tips, with every tried setting. Flat end fails on both.

## #844 (crankshaft engagement)

- `testdata/examples/crankshaft-simultaneous` with the guide's two operations (Rough: `rotary_roughing`, Z1 O-flute 3.175x25, stepdown 1; Finish: `simultaneous_finishing`, Z1 ball 3.175x22, defaults): generates in 56 s; finish 17 057 moves. Warnings: inside corners 0.66 mm, holder not checked (2.2 deg), Rough tool length. **No engagement or flute-length warning.**
- Finish alone (no Rough): only the inside-corners and holder warnings. No warning about depth.
- Engagement estimate (`tools/engage.py`, 0.3 mm voxels, stock 38 mm cylinder, poses every 0.3 mm, length of flute in material along the tool axis):
  - Finish after Rough: 6 957 of 16 751 feed moves touch material; 163 engage 1 mm or more of flute, 10 engage 3.175 mm or more, max 11.3 mm (a few voxels, slivers at the shoulders). The guide's order keeps the finish light.
  - Finish alone on the bar: 13 255 moves engage 1 mm or more, 8 643 one diameter (3.175 mm) or more, 2 771 16 mm or more, max 21.5 mm of the 22 mm flute (X 17-23, the first pin and web). This is the "whole depth of a face": the finish run without roughing, which nothing flags because the flute check (`crates/openchuck-path/src/flutes.rs`, decision 0509/0522) only warns past the flute length (21.5 < 22).
- Engagement measures in the code: `openchuck-path/src/flutes.rs` `deepest` (stock above the tip along the axis, against the setup's stock or a conservative bound; warning only past the flutes); `openchuck-testkit/src/engagement.rs` (width of cut, tests only, tool over the axis); facing's own engagement checks (decision 0547); `openchuck-sim` `DexelStock::cut` returns removed volume per pose set and `first_material`, which could carry an in-process measure. OI-1373 (open) is the engagement-limit item.
- Recommendation: keep #844 separate from #710. Different mechanism (stock state and operation order vs. edge geometry and the move test), different code (sim/flutes/app notices vs. `simultaneous.rs` refine), needs an owner decision on thresholds and a new requirement (issue says so), and applies to every strategy. One cheap overlap could ride with #710 option A only if the owner wants it: a warning when a finishing operation is the first cut in a setup and its deepest cut (from `flutes::deepest`) exceeds e.g. one tool diameter (S, 1-2 days, no golden change, notice only). Full #844 (in-process engagement from the simulation, a limit setting, multi-depth passes) is M-L (2-3 weeks) and belongs in 2027.1 with OI-1373.
